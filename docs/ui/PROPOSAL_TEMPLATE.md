# Layout proposal template

Use this to propose a way to make the Crunch Week screens feel more intentional (issue #96). Copy it, fill it in, and attach it to the issue with your images. Keep it to one page so the team can compare proposals quickly at the meeting.

## 1. Name and author

Name of the proposal, and who made it.

## 2. The idea in one sentence

What should a student see first, and what can wait one tap away?

## 3. Screens

Attach a screenshot or mockup of each screen. Say which one is the home screen. A hand sketch is fine.

## 5. Navigation

How does the student move around (tabs, rows that open, a button, something else)? How many taps to reach each part?

## 6. Colour and style rules

Where is colour used, and what does it mean? Which parts share one card style?

## 7. Effort and who could build it

Small, medium or large, and the main pieces of work. Which pieces could be built in parallel by different people?

## 8. Tests affected

Which existing tests render the screens this changes? What would have to move or be rewritten, and why?

## 9. Trade-offs

What does this make better, and what does it cost?

## 10. Questions for the team

What do you want the team to decide?
