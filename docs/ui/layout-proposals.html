Crunch Week: layout proposals

Two starting points for making the screens feel more intentional (issue #96). A third, a home screen made of a list of summary rows, was dropped because it still read as a list. They use the real sample data (Fall 2026, week 3, 12 of 15 hours this week, the next heavy week is week 6). They answer the same question differently: what should a student see first, and what can wait one tap away? Task names, hours and bar heights are scaled from that sample data (week 6 is 26.5 hours, shown as 27). These are mockups, not code.

This week tab

What you see first

  • This week's hours, the 15-week strip, and the single action to take.

Behind a tap

  • Heads-up notices, the course spike summary, the per-week detail, the syllabus reminder, and course attendance (Courses tab).

Colour rule

  • Load colours only on bars and the busy pill. One accent colour for the action. Everything else is neutral.

Trade-offs

  • Notices need one extra tap. Familiar pattern, so little to learn.

Semester tab (forecast and spikes)

What you see first

  • What to do now: three tasks, one heavy-week banner, and the bell with its count. The first screen is about action, not analysis.

Behind a tap

  • The forecast chart and course spikes (Semester tab), notices (the bell), the rest of the week's tasks, and the syllabus reminder (inside the bell).

Colour rule

  • Load colours only on the chart and the hours figure. One accent colour for the banner.

Build effort

  • Small to medium. The This week screen already exists; the main work is the tab bar, the bell, and moving the Semester sections.

Trade-offs

  • Fits how people use a phone (what do I do now?), but the semester-wide view is no longer the first impression, and the app's signature chart sits behind a tab.
